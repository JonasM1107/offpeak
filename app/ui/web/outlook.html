How good is each forecast?

The machine learning models (scikit-learn, offpeak/forecast/model.py) learn from the past year how each hour differs from the week's average. The weather version also knows the forecast for wind in the north and sun in the south, as it was forecast at the time. Every forecast goes through the same backtest, and each day ahead uses the one that misses least.

What the model learned

How this is measured: the model is trained without the last weeks, then forecasts them twice, once as usual and once with one kind of information shuffled between the hours, so it no longer fits. The bar is how much worse the forecast gets. Wind, sun and temperature are forecasts as they were known at the time (offpeak/forecast/insight.py).
