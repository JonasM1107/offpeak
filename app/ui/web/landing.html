Offpeak Intelligence

It sees next week's prices before the market does.

A machine-learning model forecasts prices up to a week ahead from past prices and the weather forecast (wind in the north, sun in the south), checked every day against simple rules. offpeak plans every machine around it.

The problem

The same kilowatt-hour cost *12 cents* at lunchtime and *53 cents* at 7 p.m. Big companies have energy managers for that. Small businesses have a monthly invoice.

offpeak is the energy manager for everyone else.

How it works

Three steps from
market price to money saved.

Prices in

Every quarter-hour of the day-ahead market, plus grid fees, levies and tax: the price you really pay. And the weather, for your own solar and wind.

A plan

For every machine that can wait, offpeak finds the cheapest start within the hours you allow, and uses your own sun and wind first.

Savings

Your week plan says which machine, when, and what it saves. The savings replay checks it against real prices of the last 30 days.
